KALMAR HAUSA

Ruwa

/rúːwà/ · Suna

Water; wani ruwa mai muhimmanci ga rayuwa.

MISALI A CIKIN JIMLA
HausaIna shan ruwa kowace rana.
I drink water every day.

Wannan samfurin gwaji ne. Za a faɗaɗa ƙamus da ma'anoni da aka tantance yayin gina aikin.
